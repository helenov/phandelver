### Sintonização

* requires Attunement by a Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, or Wizard

### Propriedades

* **Defense.** While holding the staff, you gain a +1 bonus to your AC.

####

* **Charges.** The staff has 10 charges and regains 1d6+4 expended charges daily
  at dawn. If you expend the last charge from the staff, roll a d20: on a 1, the
  staff shatters into glass shards and is destroyed.

####

* **Spells.** While holding it, you can expend its charges to cast one of the
  following spells from it (using the spell's normal casting time):
  * _Mage Armor_: 1 charge
  * _Shield_: 2 charges

####
